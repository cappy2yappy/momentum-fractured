## 📏 Technical Specs for Enemies

### Frame Size
- **Minimum:** 64×64 per frame
- **Recommended:** 128×128 (for quality)
- **Max:** 256×256 (overkill, performance hit)

### Animation Frame Counts
- Idle: 4-6 frames (looping)
- Walk: 6-8 frames (looping)
- Attack: 4-6 frames (non-looping)
- Death: 6-8 frames (non-looping, fade out)
- Special: 4-8 frames (dash, teleport, slam)

### FPS Playback
- 12 fps (smooth, cinematic)
- 24 fps (very smooth, more frames needed)
- 8 fps (retro, choppy but stylized)

**Recommendation:** 12 fps for balance (48-72 total frames per enemy)
