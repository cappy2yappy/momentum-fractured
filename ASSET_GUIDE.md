## 📜 Licensing Quick Guide

### CC0 (Public Domain)
- ✅ Use anywhere, no attribution needed
- ✅ Modify however you want
- ✅ Commercial use OK
- **Sources:** Kenney.nl, some OpenGameArt

### CC-BY (Attribution)
- ✅ Commercial use OK
- ⚠️ Must credit the artist (in game credits)
- ✅ Can modify
- **Sources:** OpenGameArt, some itch.io

### Personal/Commercial License
- ✅ Commercial use OK
- ❌ Can't resell the assets themselves
- ✅ Can use in unlimited games
- **Sources:** GameDev Market, itch.io paid, Unity Asset Store

### **Red Flags (Avoid):**
- "Personal use only" (can't sell the game)
- "Single project" licenses (can't reuse)
- Unclear licensing (email artist first)

## 🎨 Art Direction for FRACTURED

**Style Guide:**
- Modern 2D (NOT pixel art unless retro)
- Metroid Dread anime proportions
- Tokyo Noir palette: warm amber (#d4a056), red (#e94560), deep indigo (#1a1a2e)
- Smooth gradients + rim lighting
- Flowing cloth/hair physics

**When searching for assets:**
- ✅ Look for "anime style", "modern 2D", "high-res sprites"
- ❌ Avoid "8-bit", "retro", "pixel art" unless it's VERY high-res pixel art (64x64+ per character)
- ✅ Japanese/Asian themed
- ❌ Neon cyberpunk (we're Tokyo Noir, not Blade Runner)
