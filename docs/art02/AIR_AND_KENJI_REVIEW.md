# Air studies and Kenji concept review

## Aria Air pose/effect board

New review-only studies show a grounded palm gesture, aerial movement, shielding, a restrained conditioned counterpart, isolated Air marks and silhouette readability. These follow the brief's Air attack/aerial/shielding priorities. They are not new approved mechanics, attack names, damage rules, boss phases or animation frames.

Proposed effect vocabulary: pale ivory leading edge, muted teal secondary arc, broad open negative space, directional crescents and airflow streaks. Fire remains compact orange/amber tongues; Water and Darkness designs have not been developed.

At small gameplay size, the current Air arcs are too detailed to treat as finished effects. Simplify their interior streaks, preserve a clear directional edge and keep them clear of faces/limb joints in future production. Shield geometry is a visual exploration only. Airborne pose must later fit verified gameplay motion. The small conditioned pose does not establish exact device visibility; final full-body model governs placement. Some foreshortened hands and legs need artist review.

## Kenji — three new directions

All use active Kaze's purple-hair/green-accent artwork as family reference, and approved Aria as cast-material context. Kenji remains a relaxed, playful, protective, exceptionally capable 22-year-old Fire elemental. Warm tan complexion and facial resemblance are proposed, not finally approved.

| Option | Design intent | Practical review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| K4 Street Ember | Rust bomber, plum shirt and relaxed stance express warmth and confidence; shifts to danger through focused expression. | Recommended starting point; strong warm torso block. Shorten any tie and simplify folds before production. |
| K5 Clan Runner | Purple crossover workwear with ivory diagonal insert connects modern attire to subtle shinobi influence. | Clearest clan language. Keep sleeve/waist layers economical and remove any unwanted strap detail. |
| K6 Field Brother | Compact charcoal field jacket and brick shirt support the experienced mission investigator. | Practical but darker silhouette needs background-contrast review. Extra leg protection is a proposal requiring selection. |

Each includes field appearance, civilian thumbnail, expressions, silhouette and Fire gesture study. Costume variants are justified by off-duty and clan-mission contexts. No armor covered in flames or new weapon mechanics are adopted.

Creator steering during this pass: preferred swept-back loc hairstyle with closely cut/shaved sides, based on a supplied portrait. Applied across the v02 board while keeping the outfit choices. The reference was used for hair only; jewelry, formal clothing and its face are not new canon. Hair preference does not approve a full Kenji costume or final face. Simplify locs into grouped moving masses for production; fine shaved lines are secondary detail.

K4/K5/K6 require creator selection and approval. Earlier concepts are preserved; they are not sprite-production authorities. Kenji's model sheet, exact palette, scale and animations follow selection.
