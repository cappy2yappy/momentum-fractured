## Approved premise
Kaze grows up among elemental practitioners in a concealed refuge while attending school outside it. Most practitioners command a single element. A rare incarnation in her bloodline, appearing approximately every 500 years, can use and combine all elements. A clan trial reveals that Kaze is this incarnation, placing expectations on her that she initially resists.
She wants to be a hero. Against the clan's prohibition, she wears a costume and uses her abilities outside the village to help people. These acts expose the community's general vicinity to Darkness.

## Kaze
Confirmed: girl, school-age; elemental clan upbringing; heroic instinct; reluctance toward imposed destiny; rare all-element potential; existing approved appearance.
School is backdrop. Ninja presentation expresses her interests and heroic identity; do not assume the entire clan is a conventional ninja clan.
Open: exact age, personal relationships, personality beyond established traits, speech policy, response after attack, power mastery and emotional resolution.

## Darkness
Believed dead after the previous confrontation; secretly survived and spent centuries hiding, hunting elemental practitioners and building wealth. Absorbs their life essence to increase power. Enhanced elemental sensing cannot pinpoint the protected refuge.
Now a wealthy corporate leader whose true identity is unknown. Seeks destruction of Kaze's bloodline to prevent a future opponent. Kaze aims eventually to seal him for a proposed 10,000 years; killing him does not solve the lasting threat.
Open: public name, corporation name/holdings, ideology, longevity mechanics, exact absorption limits, prior battle outcome beyond survival, relationship to The Architect.

## Mizuki
Elemental parents left clan life. Darkness found and killed them and took Mizuki. She now serves him as an infiltrator.
Darkness dispatches her to the vicinity exposed by Kaze's rescues. Clan members leave the refuge, creating opportunities for contact. Her elemental presence alone does not expose allegiance.
Her friendship with Kaze becomes genuine. Seeing Kaze help people undermines her hostile assumptions. Both girls live under choices others have made for them.
Water affinity and the later betrayal / masked-helper arc are confirmed in [ST01_NARRATIVE_CANON_UPDATE.md](ST01_NARRATIVE_CANON_UPDATE.md), which supersedes the original open questions about her affinity, betrayal, expectations and later allegiance. Her knowledge of her parents' fate and Darkness's exact lies remain open; accountability is not automatic forgiveness.

## Community and conflict
The refuge masks signatures; secrecy and the ban on outside powers protect the community. Kaze's immediate compassion conflicts with collective safety.
Attack devastates the clan. Kaze survives and later finds other surviving elemental trainees. Their identities remain open.
Enemy direction: ninja forces serving Darkness. Detailed factions, recruitment and corruption are undeveloped.

## Themes supported by interview
Chosen life versus inherited responsibility; compassion under secrecy; manipulation versus genuine friendship; consequences of actions; power gained by consuming others.
Proposal: growth through learning and cooperation contrasted with absorption. Do not frame helping people as inherently wrong; antagonist chooses violence.

## Scope boundaries
Separate universe from The Elements. No playable elemental ensemble or school simulation. Preserve movement, character art, regions, boss identities, saves and unlocks.
Literal reality fracture, historical fractured-soul premise, The Refuge hub, six original biomes and The Architect remain unreconciled. Do not claim the opening or ending is complete.
