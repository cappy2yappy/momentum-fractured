# Fallen clan discovery proposals

**All options are new proposals. No victim, death scene or identity is approved by this brief.** The creator requested exploration of bodies; that authorizes this design work, not canonical casualties. Approve at most one initial casualty treatment, then assess tone in context. No named character, parent, distinctive hairstyle, signature weapon or recognizable costume can be used.

## F1 — Anonymous clan-associated casualty (recommended for review, not production)
- **Location / beat:** R8 Relay Undercroft, B06, off the checkpoint approach. Level must first show a safe standing area separated from checkpoint, guard, sigil, door and fall recovery bounds. If none fits, defer the beat; do not enlarge geometry under a story ticket.
- **Why it matters:** the search stops being an abstract goal for a moment. Kaze cannot help this person, but that does not settle anyone else's fate. It is one loss, not evidence that the whole clan died.
- **Identity:** unnamed, unidentifiable. Creator may approve clan affiliation for the internal story while Kaze recognizes only a possible communal mending practice. Do not supply age, family, rank, cause of death, precise time, a last stand or an escape narrative.
- **Recognition:** the same *type* of repair as B02, not necessarily the same cloth or maker. B06 works without B02. This proposed material culture needs approval independently of the body.
- **Examination:** optional at ordinary gameplay scale, from one respectful distance. One panel; no rotate/zoom/search-the-body verb, anatomical detail, blood effect or death animation. Do not give a nameplate or item glow.
- **Immediate objective:** unchanged survivor search and existing sigil route. No revenge quest, corpse count, health reward, loot or gate.
- **Memorial:** separate optional “Lay the cloth straight” interaction. A subtle cloth-state change only, using `st05.b06.memorial`. No mandatory prayer, new clan ritual, camera takeover or new Kaze animation unless separately commissioned and approved. Text-only fade between cloth variants is sufficient for review; production method belongs to Art/Gameplay.
- **Ambiguity:** actual death itself must be explicitly approved; do not use a fake death/revival twist. Identity, timing, cause, relationship to the district evacuation and how the person arrived remain unknown. Uncertainty must not imply a living injured person is being ignored.

## F2 — Anonymous person, clan affiliation withheld
Alternative to F1 at the same R8 location, not a second body. Remove familiar stitching and all clan clues. Kaze recognizes human loss, not a known individual. Use the alternate B06 line in WRITING.md. Examination, memorial, persistence and unchanged objective follow F1. This still introduces a death and needs creator approval; it is not an automatically safe substitute. It contributes emotional weight but does not establish a fallen clan member.

## F3 — Evidence of attempted care, no body
Alternative if the creator declines a casualty in this section. R15 Lower Sluice, near the existing dry-pump note, away from the optional steam bay: folded bandaging beside a carrying frame. Proposed reaction: **Someone needed help here. I don't know what happened next.** Optional examine, `st05.f3.read`, shared persistence contract. No loot, injury detail, blood trail, confirmed survivor or death. No memorial interaction. This is possible injury/evacuation evidence, not proof of the recent clan attack. Adds a new implied injury and therefore still requires approval.

If F3 replaces B06, the spine stays at twelve beats: mark B06 omitted and F3 as its alternate slot in the final implementation brief. Do not ship F1, F2 and F3 together to amplify tragedy by default. If all are declined, ship an eleven-beat proposal with no casualty/injury content.

## Tone / review acceptance
Use stillness, distance and Kaze's brief acknowledgement. Keep combat and reward feedback away from examination. No horror sting, dramatic death reenactment, accusatory line, collectible sparkle or achievement. Optional reduced-intensity presentation may substitute covered silhouette/text after explicit creator approval; it must convey equivalent information and cannot be assumed to exist already.

Creator signs off separately on: existence of a death; clan affiliation; recognition motif; location; visible coverage; reaction; memorial; any accessibility variant. An approving response to the overall bible must not be read as approval of all eight choices. Body artwork stays blocked until those choices are recorded.
