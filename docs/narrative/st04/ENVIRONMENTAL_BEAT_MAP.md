## The player should understand without reading lore
1. **I survived something terrible.** Kaze is alone in a damaged, navigable district, but this is not her destroyed clan refuge.
2. **People once used these spaces.** Civilian stalls, public waterworks, worker circulation and maintenance markers lend human scale, without proving current residents fled during the refuge attack.
3. **There may be a route to people beyond here.** Archive and pumps are connected infrastructure; restoring passage provides reach rather than an electronic survivor scanner.
4. **I have choices and recovery paths.** Navigable optional routes, supported ledges, landmark lighting, return loops and checkpoints preserve the Metroidvania's exploratory agency.
