## Chronology: authoritative and spoiler-separated
1. **CONFIRMED, before the attack:** The hidden clan trains elemental practitioners and masks their signatures. Kaze's clan trial reveals rare all-element potential. Members still travel outside; using powers publicly violates clan secrecy.
2. **CONFIRMED, pre-attack exposure:** Kaze's compassionate outside rescues leave traces pointing Darkness toward the *general vicinity*, not the precise concealed refuge. Darkness sends Mizuki to infiltrate the community; a real friendship develops.
3. **CONFIRMED, attack:** Mizuki discloses the actual location while expecting Kaze's capture rather than the clan's massacre. Darkness attacks. Kaze's parents sacrifice themselves to save her; she escapes and loses consciousness. Kenji is absent on his assignment investigating disappearances and searching for Aria; his departure's relative timing within the infiltration is not pinned down.
4. **CONFIRMED, opening playable district:** Kaze awakens alone and enters the current 21-room damaged district **immediately after the attack**. She must survive, seek safer routes and look for other clan survivors, **not** set out for immediate revenge. She does not yet know every survivor's fate.
5. **IMPLEMENTED progression + PROPOSED story interpretation:** Market/abilities, shrine/traversal, Warden, Archive relay, annexes/sigils, SkyWorks, foundry, Keeper and returns already define the playable route. Repairing relays can be explained as reopening passage and extending Kaze's search; it is **not** a new communications device or confirmed survivor locator.
6. **CONFIRMED for later story, timing/locations not this section:** Some trainees survive. Kenji eventually reunites with Kaze. Aria's captivity and eventual nonlethal rescue, Mizuki's simultaneous masked-helper/betrayal disclosure, and a later forest sanctuary occur on their own approved arcs, not as unannounced R0–R20 reveals.

## Spoiler boundaries for player-facing opening
Player may understand: her refuge was attacked; she escaped; she is alone now; she needs to find survivors and a way through.
Do **not** disclose: Mizuki's involvement, a masked helper identity, Aria's location/conditioning, a specific surviving trainee's whereabouts, Darkness's hidden public identity/ArcuTech control, or a future sanctuary already operating.
Keep Kaze's parental sacrifice consistent with a future/prologue portrayal; this minimal in-game opening need not recap every known event.
