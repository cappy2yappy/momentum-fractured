## Safe presentation contract — future owner validation
- New game: proposed intro only after creator/Level approval. Do not replace boot/load flow from this planning ticket.
- Skip at any intro card: request cancellation through accepted owner, release once; world supplies the identical safe arrival and exact objective. Presenter never sets flags/checkpoints/room requests. No prologue replay on continue save.
- Pause/menu/focus loss: suspend presentation clock, suppress surprise audio/auto-advance; on return offer resume or skip with single handback. Do not silently resume controller during retained cinematic input lease.
- Damage/death/room rebuild: authoritative world cancels stale presentation. No end callback can teleport, resurrect, re-grant or overwrite new control owner.
- Remapped keyboard/controller: resolve actual bindings at display time; no hardcoded Shift/Q/R in new art. Provide text alternatives; never audio-only or color-only evidence.
- Reduced motion: no shake, tracking, parallax, speedline/flicker; stable cards with equivalent information. High-intensity flashes omitted. Text scaling and high contrast, no baked-in lettering.
- Fire: credible unsafe heat/standing/return conditions need Level/HAZ geometry and safe retreat. Do not paste flame artwork onto a climbable safe wall and call it blocked. Neither disable earned abilities nor grant later abilities early.
- Existing Canal Doorway and automatic transitions keep ownership. No first-evidence prompt overlaps a passage, checkpoint, pickup, enemy/hazard or rope envelope.
These are specifications awaiting implementation tests, not proof of CIN/INT support.
