# Narrative Structure — Working Framework
Status: partial foundation; scene order, acts and ending are proposals, not approved plot.

## Established causal sequence
Clan upbringing and ordinary outside life → Kaze's elemental trial and imposed responsibility → secret heroic use outside refuge → Darkness locates general vicinity → Mizuki contacts/infiltrates community → genuine friendship challenges her indoctrination → attack devastates clan → Kaze survives and later meets surviving trainees → eventual objective of sealing Darkness.
Relative order of trial, first rescues and Mizuki's arrival requires confirmation; do not lock a cinematic chronology from this sequence.

## Unresolved dramatic beats
Opening scene and playable start; immediate post-attack goal; first revelation of corporate identity; revelation of Mizuki's role; surviving trainees' introductions; midpoint reversal; personal revelation; sealing confrontation and resolution.
No staged betrayal, rescue, confession, redemption, villain reveal or ending has been approved.

## Exploration constraints
Tell story within current ability-gated city. Optional clues may deepen interpretation without required new save gates. School may appear as brief background context; no school mechanics.
Proposal: distribute conflicting records and survivors' accounts across regions, rather than requiring chronological exposition. Preserve mandatory progression.
Sealing versus killing is the intended distinction; ritual ingredients, price, success conditions and player choices are open.
