# Narrative Structure — Working Framework
Updated 2026-11-08. Confirmed events and creator-directed opening concept are distinguished from unresolved staging and runtime placement. See [KAZE_STORY_BIBLE.md](KAZE_STORY_BIBLE.md) and [ST01_NARRATIVE_CANON_UPDATE.md](ST01_NARRATIVE_CANON_UPDATE.md).

## Established world history
In an antiquity-like era before modern technology, Darkness threatens a populated human civilization. The former all-element wielder fights to seal him. Darkness intends to absorb the wielder and keep him alive until that happens. After hand-to-hand, elemental and aerial combat, Darkness targets the people to force the wielder to divide his strength. The wielder chooses protection and sacrifices his life to save them. Darkness gains the advantage but accidentally kills him before absorption. Furious at the lost power, Darkness vows to wait for reincarnation, then leaves in anger before the seal can close. The sacrifice's exact technique and relationship to the seal remain open. Darkness is believed dead, but lives continuously through the next 5,000 years. His powers help him accumulate wealth and influence; in modern times he secretly leads ArcuTech. Propaganda portraying elementals as cruel and dangerous helps drive survivors into concealment.

Exactly four full elementals exist in the present-day world: Kaze (all-element potential), Mizuki (Water), Kenji (Fire) and Aria (Air). The creator explicitly confirmed Kaze is included in this total. Kaze's villagers have weaker wind remnants. The clan believes its community is the last left, within the limits of its knowledge. The public knows ArcuTech without knowing its hidden truth; the clan has suspicions and rumors. Initially Darkness has secured only Mizuki, whose allegiance he obtained through deception and personal manipulation.

## Established causal sequence
Kaze's clan upbringing and outside school life, trial and imposed responsibility, and secret rescues establish her compassion and conflict with clan rules. The rescues expose elemental traces and the general vicinity. Mizuki infiltrates, develops a genuine friendship and ultimately reveals the refuge location while expecting Kaze's capture rather than a massacre. Darkness attacks and overwhelms Kaze. Her parents arrange her escape and die protecting her. She loses consciousness, survives and begins looking for survivors.

Relative order of the trial, first rescues and Mizuki's arrival remains open. The sequence above describes author-facing causes, not the order in which the player learns the secrets. Surviving trainees are not automatically extra full elementals.

## Current intro-cutscene direction
The creator requested an illustrated opening with these beats:

1. Extended ancient fight: hand-to-hand combat, elemental attacks and an aerial clash above the people. Establish the wielder's sealing objective and Darkness's intention to absorb him alive. Darkness forces the choice between protecting the people and fighting at full power. The wielder chooses protection, gives up his combat advantage and dies saving them. Darkness realizes his accidental kill prevented absorption, reacts angrily, vows to wait for reincarnation and escapes through the incomplete seal. Give each turn a distinct readable beat.
2. A clear "5,000 years later" transition: establish modern ArcuTech and the world shaped by propaganda.
3. Kaze's ordinary life: school, concealed village and training, with appropriate costume/time continuity.
4. Attack and collapse: preserve the parents' protective sacrifice within the destruction sequence.
5. Fall into a chasm and unconsciousness: exact protection and survivable landing remain unresolved.
6. Battered recovery beneath the temple/refuge, exploration toward light and the desired handoff to gameplay.

Ancient-page visual direction: clean shonen anime linework and cel shading inspired by Naruto/MHA, but with a darker, restrained palette. Human defenders and civilians are present; prehistoric-looking animals remain proposed fantasy scenery. The exact protective effect is storyboard exploration, not a locked ability or sealing mechanism.

This is the current cinematic concept. Page/panel count, narration, timing, what the audience knows about ArcuTech's leader and reconciliation with existing exploration/later-flashback presentation remain open. No runtime sequence or save/progression change follows automatically.

## Reveal and continuity constraints
- Conceal Mizuki's betrayal and masked-helper identity from Kaze and the player until their later simultaneous revelation. Do not show her giving Darkness the refuge location in the opening.
- Her parents' murder and Darkness's plan to consume her are author-facing truths; their reveal timing is not automatically the prologue.
- Aria eventually becomes Darkness's captive and an ArcuTech-conditioned opponent used to bait Kaze. Do not establish her as a second captive in the initial modern setup, where Darkness has only Mizuki. Her disappearance can precede a confirmed capture date; the date is open.
- The four-person roster is confirmed: Kaze, Mizuki, Kenji and Aria. Kaze counts within the four. Kenji remains Kaze's older brother and the Fire elemental; Aria remains Air. Do not invent another full elemental to complete an assumed classical-element set.
- The approximately 500-year incarnation tradition remains a history question within the 5,000-year span. Do not invent an intervening cast.
- Kaze initially seeks and rescues survivors. Her eventual objective is sealing Darkness; the ritual, price, duration, success conditions and ending remain open.

## Exploration constraints
Tell the story within the approved ability-gated world and preserve mandatory progression. Optional clues may deepen interpretation without requiring new save gates. School is background; no school simulation is implied.
Proposal: distribute conflicting records and survivors' accounts across regions. This supports the propaganda theme without treating invented documents or encounters as already approved.
