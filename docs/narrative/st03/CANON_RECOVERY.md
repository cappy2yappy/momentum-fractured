## Naming continuity
The assignment says **Mikaze** and **Mentals**. Recovered creator interview/latest canon consistently use **Mizuki** and **elemental practitioners / elementals**. ST-03 treats the assignment terms as unresolved aliases, not authorization to rename characters or create a new species. Kaze remains Kaze; no inferred relationship is discarded. Creator confirmation is requested before any naming migration.

## Confirmed canon
- Kaze is 16, compassionate, lively and impulsive; a clan-trained elemental practitioner who wants to help people. School is background; costume/ninja interests are part of her secret heroic expression.
- Most users have one affinity. Kaze's bloodline has rare all-element potential, approximately a 500-year cycle. Trial reveals affinity; exact maturity/test rules remain open.
- Refuge masks elemental signatures, but members leave for school and ordinary life. Outside power use is forbidden for secrecy.
- Kaze's rescues expose elemental traces, narrowing Darkness's search to a **general vicinity**. He cannot pinpoint the protected refuge. He sends Mizuki to contact/infiltrate this community.
- Mizuki's parents left clan life; Darkness killed them and took/raised her through manipulation. Her friendship with Kaze becomes genuine; she discloses the refuge believing Kaze will be captured, not the clan massacred.
- Attack: parents die protecting Kaze; she escapes, loses consciousness and awakens afterward. Some trainees survive. Her initial goal is survivor rescue, not revenge. New forest sanctuary is a later survivor-network achievement, not an existing room assignment.
- Darkness survived the previous confrontation, hidden for centuries; consumes elemental essence and heads ArcuTech. Do not restore the superseded synchronized-reincarnation explanation.
- Kenji, 22, is Kaze's older Fire-aligned brother; absent on disappearance investigation/search for Aria. Original manuscript childhood parental fire is superseded.
- Aria, Air-aligned, is Kenji's close friend with emerging romance; captive and technologically conditioned to bait Kaze; later rescued nonlethally. Mizuki's choices/grooming are distinct from Aria's imposed control.
- Mizuki flees Darkness, aids Kaze in disguise, and her helper identity/betrayal are revealed together later; uneasy alliance is not automatic forgiveness.
- Long-term sealing is intended, with proposed 10,000-year duration; exact ritual/rules open.

## Exposure chain — preserve causality
Protected village + ordinary outside lives → Kaze helps people using powers → detectable traces reveal vicinity → Mizuki approaches members outside → infiltration reveals refuge → Darkness chooses devastating attack.
Do not replace this with a trial beacon, omniscient sensing, public village address, Kaze knowingly betraying the clan, or ancient artifacts locating the present refuge.
Post-attack regional exploration does not retroactively cause the original exposure.
