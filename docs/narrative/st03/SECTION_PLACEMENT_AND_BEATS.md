# ST-03 Current Section Placement, Objectives and Beats
Status: **proposed mapping onto existing gameplay**, except explicitly identified creator decisions.

## Recommendation
Use the 21-room district as the first substantial **post-attack survival-and-search section**, following the clan prologue and parental escape. Kaze wakes alone (conversation direction), finds a usable route through damaged waterfront/industrial ruins and searches for evidence that people can still move through them.
Treat "ruins escape" as gaining safety and navigational agency, not a compulsory one-way dash out of every room. It is not the attack itself and not yet the Aria rescue facility.

### Alternatives evaluated
| Placement | Fit | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Before the attack | Supports ordinary hero activity but contradicts proposed solitary wake/survival framing | Not recommended for the whole 21-room section |
| Immediately after escape from attack | Supports isolation, limited starting abilities, rescue goal and reopened routes | Recommended; room/shelter assignment needs approval |
| Much later inside ArcuTech | Could support industrial scenery but assumes unrevealed ownership and risks forcing Aria into current bosses | Defer; not inferred from scenery |

## Minimum player knowledge
On entering: Kaze survived an attack, family/community are missing or lost, she needs a safe way onward. Keep prologue knowledge consistent; avoid amnesia unless approved.
On leaving the section's current main-route milestone: she has restored access through a real district, knows its roof/water/service systems connect, and has reason to continue searching rather than assume she alone survived.
Proof that a particular survivor is alive, a reunion, corporate identity reveal, Mizuki reveal, Aria location and a new exit beyond the 21 rooms are **not approved/in scope**.

## Environmental information layers
1. Physical survival: readable landings, shelter silhouettes, hazards and route landmarks.
2. Older history: proposed ancient affinity-reactive inscriptions/artifacts, weathered plans and architecture.
3. Existing undated working history: evacuation/convoy and maintenance records; dating pending.
4. Possible recent use: lamps or repairs suggest care without assigning a named survivor.

Modern-looking active terminals cannot simply be declared centuries old. Proposal: durable engraved/elemental record devices inside later maintenance housings. Their reaction recognizes affinity and reveals optional text; no identity scanner, quest item, inventory requirement or new map edge.
Optional details enrich the mystery; a player who skips text can still follow the main route.
