# ST-03 Current Section Placement, Objectives and Beats
Status: **post-attack placement and survivor-first motive confirmed October 16**; exact room-specific staging, text, clues and beat-to-mechanic interpretation remain proposals.

## Confirmed placement and proposed framing
**Creator-confirmed:** The current 21-room district is immediately **after** the concealed refuge attack. The prologue/assault and parental sacrifice precede this district; Kaze escapes, loses consciousness, awakens alone, and seeks safety and other survivors before revenge.
**Proposed presentation:** Make canal-to-relay exploration feel like a practical attempt to reopen blocked passage, light, access or routing so Kaze can reach farther places where survivors might have gone. The infrastructure does **not** identify a living survivor or automatically transmit their position. Kaze's outward progress stays a search, not a revenge mission.
**Open:** The exact awakening point/emergency shelter relationship to R0, when/why specific district structures were damaged, who used them recently, artifact behavior, and the section-end evidence. Do not equate the district with the original clan refuge or the future sanctuary.

## Minimum player knowledge
On entering: Kaze knows the concealed refuge was attacked; her parents gave their lives for her escape. She is separated from her people and must search for survivors. Do not imply she forgot the attack or knows the fate of every clan member.
On leaving the section's current main-route milestone: she has restored usable routes across a real district and can continue searching beyond formerly blocked areas. This is **not proof a particular person survived** and not the establishment of the future sanctuary.
Proof that a particular survivor is alive, a reunion, corporate identity reveal, Mizuki reveal, Aria location and a new exit beyond the 21 rooms are **not approved/in scope**.

## Environmental information layers
1. Physical survival: readable landings, shelter silhouettes, hazards and route landmarks.
2. Older history: proposed ancient affinity-reactive inscriptions/artifacts, weathered plans and architecture.
3. Existing undated working history: evacuation/convoy and maintenance records; dating pending.
4. Possible recent use: lamps or repairs suggest care without assigning a named survivor.

Modern-looking active terminals cannot simply be declared centuries old. Proposal: durable engraved/elemental record devices inside later maintenance housings. Their reaction recognizes affinity and reveals optional text; no identity scanner, quest item, inventory requirement or new map edge.
Optional details enrich the mystery; a player who skips text can still follow the main route.
