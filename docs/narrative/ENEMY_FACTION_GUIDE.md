# Enemy and Faction Guide — Foundation
## Creator-confirmed direction
Darkness commands ninja forces. He hunts elemental practitioners and absorbs life essence. Clan practitioners are not inherently hostile. Other elemental trainees survive and support the story without becoming playable.
Mizuki is an infiltrator with genuine emerging friendships, not a generic enemy archetype.

## Current implementation evidence
Ordinary guards/sentinels reuse existing guard behavior/art. Tide Warden and Storm Keeper are existing encounters. DEV-06 adds no new commissioned boss/enemy archetype.
Boss motivations, faction allegiance and relationship to Darkness are unresolved. Preserve display names and progression flags until creative approval.

## Open faction design
Darkness's forces: origins, recruitment, goals, allegiance, silhouette, combat identity and regional presence.
Clan: name, culture, organization and single-element disciplines.
Independent elementals: identities and stance toward clan obligations.
Undead/corrupted variants, shrine guardians and corporate security remain options, not established factions.
