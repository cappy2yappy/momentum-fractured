# Enemy and Faction Guide — Current Canon
Updated 2026-11-08 from the creator's world-history and elemental-population clarification; see [KAZE_STORY_BIBLE.md](KAZE_STORY_BIBLE.md).
## Creator-confirmed direction
Darkness commands ninja forces, secretly leads ArcuTech and has survived the 5,000 years since the ancient battle. He hunts full elementals and absorbs elemental life essence; the exact restoration requirement and benefit from lesser remnants remain open.
Exactly four full elementals exist in the present-day world. Ordinary members of Kaze's hidden village possess weaker wind remnants. Surviving trainees and other practitioners must not automatically be classified as additional full elementals.
Darkness's propaganda falsely portrays elementals as cruel and dangerous, helping drive the clan into hiding. The public knows ArcuTech but not its leader's Darkness identity or the full truth of its crimes. Clan members have suspicions and rumors; their belief that they are the last surviving community is limited to what they know.
Mizuki is the Water elemental and, at the initial modern setup, the only full elemental Darkness has secured on his side. He killed her parents, manipulated her allegiance and plans eventually to consume her. She is an infiltrator whose friendships become genuine. Preserve the later simultaneous revelation of her betrayal and masked-helper identity.
Aria's later coercive captivity under ArcuTech remains established; its onset must be reconciled with the initial Mizuki-only state.
