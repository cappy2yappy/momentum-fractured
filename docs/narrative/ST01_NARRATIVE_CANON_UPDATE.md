## 2. Confirmed narrative canon

### Kaze and the prologue

- Kaze is 16, a lively, compassionate, impulsive girl with dark brown skin from a concealed elemental clan in a fictional modern-Japanese region. She wants to be a hero. Preserve her dark skin in all visual continuity.
- Exactly four full elementals exist in the present-day world. Ordinary villagers carry weaker wind remnants and do not add to that full-elemental count. Kaze carries a rare lineage with the potential to access and combine all elements. The creator explicitly confirmed the four as Kaze (all-element potential), Mizuki (Water), Kenji (Fire) and Aria (Air), with Kaze included in that total. Public sightings are generally treated as urban legends; secret interventions reveal elemental traces.
- The established attack events remain: Darkness overwhelms Kaze; her parents intervene, arrange her escape and die protecting her; she loses consciousness and awakens afterward. The earlier playable-prologue framing is now being adapted into the creator's illustrated opening direction: ancient battle, a 5,000-year jump, school/clan life, attack, collapse/fall and underground recovery toward light. Cinematic versus playable presentation and alignment with existing exploration/flashback placement remain to be resolved separately. Exact protective escape and landing mechanics remain **OPEN**; this document does not change runtime sequence.
- Kaze's initial goal is to locate and rescue clan survivors, not pursue revenge. A survivor network eventually establishes a new concealed forest sanctuary while aspiring to reclaim the original refuge.

- **PROFILE REFERENCE:** Kaze's reference-supported arcade-game interest, favorite purple and dislike of early mornings, plus proposed additions, are recorded in [her everyday profile](KAZE_STORY_BIBLE.md#kaze--likes-dislikes-and-everyday-habit). Her age remains 16 despite the older sheet's age label.

### Kenji Harima (Fire)

- **CONFIRMED:** Kenji is Kaze's 22-year-old older brother, the Fire elemental adapted from the source manuscript.
- **CONFIRMED:** He is deeply close to and protective of Kaze; relaxed and playful outside conflict, formidable and focused in battle.
- **CONFIRMED:** He was absent from the village during the attack while carrying out clan investigative work into disappearing elementals. He is searching for his missing mission partner Aria.
- **CONFIRMED:** He survives, operates beyond the village, and eventually reunites with Kaze and joins forces with her.
- **CONTINUITY OVERRIDE:** The source manuscript's account of Kenji causing the childhood fire that killed his parents **does not carry over**. In Kaze, both siblings' parents die during Darkness's attack while saving Kaze. His other source traits can be adapted subject to consistency.

- **PROPOSED EVERYDAY PROFILE:** Likes, dislikes and a food-sharing habit are in [Kenji's profile](KAZE_STORY_BIBLE.md#kenji--likes-dislikes-and-everyday-habit); these do not change his confirmed family or investigation arc.

### Aria Zephyros (Air)

- **CONFIRMED:** Aria is the Air elemental adapted from *The Elements* and Kenji's missing investigation partner. She and Kenji begin as close friends with developing romantic feelings, not an established couple.
- **CONFIRMED:** In Aria's captivity arc, Darkness holds her to bait **Kaze specifically**. Kenji searches for her. **TIMING OPEN:** The creator's current modern setup gives Darkness only Mizuki; Aria's disappearance and eventual capture must be placed consistently with that initial state. Do not establish Aria as a simultaneous second captive at the opening by default.
- **CONFIRMED:** Aria has been subjected to **technological brainwashing** by ArcuTech, deliberately distinguished from Mizuki's upbringing-based conditioning. Her memories of Kenji are suppressed during her boss encounter.
- **CONFIRMED:** Aria confronts Kaze as an antagonistic boss under external conditioning and is subsequently freed and recruited as an ally. The exact deconditioning sequence, recovery timescale and subsequent hub/field role remain **OPEN**.
- **SOURCE ADAPTATION:** Her intelligence, scientific curiosity, emotional insight, air-pressure manipulation, wind barriers, limited flight and weather influence are present in the manuscript. Gameplay implementation of any specific ability remains **PROPOSED**.

- **PROPOSED EVERYDAY PROFILE:** Likes, dislikes and a habit of getting absorbed in a mechanism are in [Aria's profile](KAZE_STORY_BIBLE.md#aria--likes-dislikes-and-everyday-habit); they remain distinct from technological conditioning and recovery.

### Darkness / ArcuTech

- **CONFIRMED (expanded creator fight direction, 2026-11-08):** The ancient fight includes hand-to-hand combat, elemental attacks and fighting above the people. The former all-element wielder states that he is fighting to seal Darkness. Darkness states his absorption goal and intends to keep the wielder alive long enough to obtain his power.
- **CONFIRMED:** Darkness deliberately attacks or threatens the people to force the wielder to choose between protecting them and fighting at full power. The wielder chooses to save them. Diverting his strength gives Darkness the advantage, but Darkness accidentally kills his intended absorption target. The sacrifice succeeds in protecting the people.
- **CONFIRMED:** Darkness is furious about killing the wielder before absorbing him, then resolves to bide his time until reincarnation and leaves angrily through the incomplete seal. This motivation leads into the 5,000-year jump to modern Kaze's story. The exact dialogue and combat choreography are drafts; failed absorption, the protective choice and the reincarnation vow are canon. General absorption mechanics remain open.
- **CONFIRMED:** The ancient battle occurs in a pre-modern, antiquity-like era. The former all-element wielder deliberately sacrifices their life to save everyone threatened by Darkness's attack. The people are saved at the cost of the wielder's life. Darkness causes the lethal attack and escapes before the sealing is completed. This is a protective sacrifice, not merely a defeat in a duel; the exact protective technique and its connection to the seal remain open. Believed dead afterward, Darkness survives continuously for the 5,000 years leading to modern day. The "ancient Egypt-type" reference is visual-era direction, not literal Earth geography or history.
- **CONFIRMED:** Darkness uses his powers over that span to accumulate extraordinary wealth and worldwide influence, eventually heading **ArcuTech**. His public corporate identity is not publicly recognized as Darkness.
- **CONFIRMED:** He hunts elusive full elementals, absorbs elemental life essence to gain power and seeks to eliminate Kaze's bloodline. He needs the remaining full elementals for a restoration objective whose exact mechanism and target state remain open. Wealth and elemental sensing have not allowed him to pinpoint the hidden refuge.
- **CONFIRMED:** His propaganda portrays elementals as cruel and dangerous and contributes to their concealment. The public knows ArcuTech without knowing its hidden truth; clan members have suspicions and rumors about wrongdoing. They believe their refuge is the last surviving community of its kind, within the limits of their knowledge.
- **CONFIRMED:** ArcuTech abducts elemental practitioners. Darkness's use of modern neurotechnology against Aria is a step beyond the prolonged grooming used against Mizuki.
- **PROPOSED:** ArcuTech publicly promotes neurorehabilitation or assistive research as cover for coercive neural manipulation; this corporate cover story needs creator approval.

- **PROPOSED EVERYDAY PROFILES:** [Darkness's private tastes](KAZE_STORY_BIBLE.md#darkness--likes-dislikes-and-everyday-habit) and the [former wielder's ancient-era preferences](KAZE_STORY_BIBLE.md#former-all-element-wielder--likes-dislikes-and-everyday-habit) are characterization proposals. ## 3. Project MNEMOSYNE: concept approval boundary

**ENDORSED CONCEPT:** The creator enthusiastically supported a near-future brain-computer-interface concept inspired loosely by Neuralink, technologically induced memory suppression, a behind-the-ear visual indicator, and a boss fight in which network disruption weakens Aria's conditioning. **Project MNEMOSYNE** is a useful **working codename**, not an explicitly ratified permanent name. A small behind-ear physical implant and ArcuTech infrastructure are the **recommended implementation**, not yet separately selected as final canon.

**Fictional limitations (PROPOSED):** The system can inhibit retrieval of selected memories, distort threat associations and issue behavioral instructions, but cannot erase a person's underlying identity or create authentic affection or moral conviction. The system preserves combat intelligence and Air techniques. This is fictional speculative technology, not a claim about current real-world brain-computer interfaces.

**Rehabilitation (PROPOSED):** Following signal disruption, Aria's memories return unevenly and she undergoes gradual recovery. This should avoid a simplistic instant personality reset. She should retain agency and help solve the aftermath.

## 4. Proposed narrative sequence (not yet locked beat-for-beat)

1. Kenji and Aria investigate elemental disappearances as a clan assignment.
2. Aria disappears while following a lead; Kenji searches for her and is absent when Darkness devastates the village. Her capture happens within the broader investigation arc, but its exact placement relative to the initial modern setup is **OPEN**: Darkness has only Mizuki at that setup. The earlier proposed automatic placement of Aria's capture before the opening is no longer fixed.
3. Kaze survives the parental sacrifice and begins survivor rescue, later reunites with Kenji. Surviving trainees and missing practitioners are not automatically additional full elementals.
4. Darkness allows an ArcuTech lead to reach the siblings, using captive Aria to bring Kaze into his chosen facility.
5. Kaze fights Aria, who retains her Air abilities but cannot remember Kenji.
6. Kaze and Kenji sever the conditioning system without killing Aria.
7. Aria gradually recovers, shares intelligence and becomes a willing ally within the survivor network.

**Spoiler rule:** Mizuki's betrayal and identity remain concealed from Kaze and the player until their later simultaneous revelation; do not use Aria's storyline to prematurely reveal it.

## 5. Continuity safeguards

- *The Elements* is an inspiration and character source, **not** automatically binding canon. Its five incarnation cycle, specific reincarnation chronology, Kenji's childhood parental deaths, Marcus Blackthorne history, Shadowpeak Industries and earlier relationship entanglements cannot be imported wholesale.
- Kaze remains the **sole playable protagonist** unless separately approved. Kenji and Aria are supporting story characters, not automatic switchable characters.
- Preserve existing approved level structure and alpha implementation. Placement of Aria's facility, new boss mechanics, upgrades, quests, flags, dialogue and cutscenes requires cross-team review.
- No forced permanent companion or mandatory elemental upgrade is approved yet.
- The present-day full-elemental total is four. Do not count ordinary wind-remnant villagers as full elementals, add an unconfirmed fifth bearer or silently change Kenji's Fire or Aria's Air affinity. Historical populations and Mizuki's parents' exact power level are not fixed by this count.
- Darkness's stated plan to wait for reincarnation is confirmed. The older approximately 500-year incarnation tradition still requires reconciliation with the 5,000-year elapsed history; neither the vow nor the time jump authorizes an invented intervening cast or proves there were no other incarnations. Darkness himself survives continuously and is not reincarnated.
