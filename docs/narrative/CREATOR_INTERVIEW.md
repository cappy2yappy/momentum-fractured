# ST-01 Creator Interview and Decision Log
Session: 2026-10-12 (America/New_York).
Publication authorized: "great lets make this documented and add it to the git".
This is a faithful paraphrase of a conversational, voice-dictated interview. Not every agent suggestion was approved.

## Confirmed decisions
D01. The Elements is inspiration; Kaze is a separate story and retains her established visual identity. Do not transplant the Fire protagonist or full school ensemble.
D02. Kaze is a school-age girl from a clan of elemental practitioners. She wants to be a hero; ninja interests and her costume shape her secret heroic activity. School is background.
D03. Most elemental users wield one element; the rare incarnation in Kaze's bloodline can wield all elements and combine them. The cycle is approximately 500 years.
D04. A clan trial reveals elemental affinity as powers mature. Kaze initially does not want the imposed responsibility. Exact age/puberty terminology remains unresolved.
D05. Darkness seeks to destroy the bloodline to prevent future incarnations that can stop him. Other elementals can cooperate against him; they are supporting, nonplayable characters.
D06. Killing Darkness cannot provide a permanent solution; a long seal is the intended objective. The proposed duration is 10,000 years; exact rules remain open.
D07. REVISED: Darkness was believed killed in the previous confrontation but survived. He has hidden for centuries, accumulated wealth, hunted elementals globally, and now leads a megacorporation under an unrecognized public identity. Reincarnation alongside Kaze is superseded.
D08. Darkness absorbs elemental life essence, increasing his power. He senses elementals more effectively than ordinary practitioners. Kaze is exceptional too; exact sensing comparison/range remains open.
D09. Elemental practitioners can sense one another. Presence does not establish loyalty.
D10. The refuge conceals elemental signatures. Members nevertheless leave for school and everyday life.
D11. Powers are forbidden outside the village and must remain secret. Kaze cannot ignore people needing help and uses her abilities in costume.
D12. Her outside rescues reveal a general vicinity, not the exact refuge. Darkness sends Mizuki to find/contact elemental members there and infiltrate their community.
D13. Mizuki serves Darkness. Her elemental parents left the clan to choose an ordinary life; Darkness detected and killed them, then took Mizuki.
D14. Mizuki and Kaze develop genuine friendship despite the mission. Witnessing Kaze's selfless help challenges what Mizuki was taught about the clan.
D15. Kaze and Mizuki reflect one another: others make choices for their lives.
D16. Attack devastates the clan; Kaze survives and eventually finds other surviving elemental trainees. Do not describe her as the only survivor or every clan member as dead.

## Agent proposals, not approved facts
- Trial automatically activates a detectable multi-element signature (superseded as the proposed primary discovery mechanism).
- Power discovery is distinct from mastery; learning can explain unlocks.
- Other elementals can fight Darkness but only combined inner harmony can produce the lasting seal.
- Absorption cannot reproduce Kaze's sealing capability.
- Corporation controls essential city services; prior corporate identities appear as ancestors.
- Mizuki expects capture rather than massacre; rescue she witnesses is unplanned.
- Kaze searches for a missing person; clan members deliberately enable her escape.
- Close-range passive sensing and farther active-power detection.
These may be discussed, not silently incorporated as canon.
