## Four layers

| Layer | Purpose | Native study contract | Artist deliverable |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Far | Place, atmosphere and repeated district rhythm | Subtle glazing in free-space cells plus faded vertical ribs; no colliders, solid-looking horizontal shelves or asserted refuge sightline | Soft painted distance plate; destination geography only after Level markup |
| 2 Mid | Explain load, maintenance and construction | Recess/inset rhythm inside existing broad masonry faces; never a bridge over a real gap | Structural backing keyed to approved support geometry |
| 3 Play | Truthful material on the usable surface | Every inlay enclosed by an actual Rect2; original top rim and underfoot/exit labels stay visible | Collision-aligned face atlas and masks; no silhouette expansion |
| 4 Fore | Sparse depth and local life | Finite hanging vegetation/cord/light envelopes outside interaction and walking masks | Separate alpha pieces with all-frame motion bounds; foreground may be omitted where no safe space exists |

Foreground is deliberately sparse. There is no quota that forces decoration into a busy room. Far/mid assets may not invent a ledge simply because their opacity is low. These studies preserve the existing backdrop; final painted structural work is separately commissioned.

## Evidence vocabulary: distinguish cause from appearance

| Read | Visual treatment proposal | Forbidden inference |
|---|---|---|
| Age / wear | Rounded edges, moss in joints, waterlines, dull fasteners | Does not date abandonment or evacuation |
| Repair | Contrasting infill, added collar, regular fastening pattern | Repair age, repairer and allegiance remain unknown |
| Break / damage | Interrupted masonry grain, exposed aggregate, asymmetrical fracture | No blanket claim that Darkness caused district damage |
| Active heat / fire | Visible fuel/source, coherent hot volume, pre-activation warning, persistent physical boundary read, safe alternative | No decorative fake damage, invisible wall or disabled wall-jump; no fire placed in this gallery |
| Rest / care | Sheltered warm accent, swept visual rhythm only after prop approval | No proof of a present survivor, named caretaker or recent visit |
| Restored power | Existing relay-state-linked lights only after event owner review | No survivor signal, reunion or faction disclosure |

## A/B/C event vocabulary

| Category | Candidate examples | Timing / accessibility / ownership | Current deliverable |
|---|---|---|---|
| A — ambient loop | Canal mist, small hanging cord sway, low-amplitude lamp variation, foliage loop | Cosmetic only. Fixed 3px cord amplitude, slow 0.8rad/s sway; lamp alpha variation ±0.015. Pause freezes clocks; reduced motion fixes frame/pose. No per-frame asset decode. | Cord/foliage/light loops in native studies. No smoke without mapped source. |
| B — local cosmetic response | Dust settling behind lane, released sign settling, lamp stabilizing after actual restoration | At most 1.8s; finite envelope; cancellable/disposable on room exit. Gameplay event is read-only and requires owner-approved hook. No collision, damage, reward, save write or dialogue. | F9 manual isolated dust preview, **not wired to any world event**. Reattach clears transient time. |
| C — hazard / physics device | Hot return volume, falling beam, rideable lift, striking debris, active steam | Separate Level + Gameplay + HAZ contract. At least existing HAZ01 warning contract, fair escape and true collision; review actual abilities, old/new saves and reciprocity. | Specification only. Zero new devices, colliders, warning timers or health changes. |

All three categories are specified per room in the matrix. “C deferred” is not a request to add a hazard. Quiet areas remain quiet; R0/R11/R16 stay safe. Revisit art cannot imply a new story state. No seeds randomize routes or meaningful props.
