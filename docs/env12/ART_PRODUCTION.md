# Artist production packet — missing illustrations, not replacements

Recipient: Art Mane with Level/Story and the existing environmental-art owner. Every new picture/placement remains creator-review pending. Source Kaze and environment artwork must remain byte-identical. No external art service receives project imagery under this assignment.

## Deliverable convention

Use a layered authoring source (Clip Studio Paint or PSD after artist selection), plus engine-friendly lossless PNG layer exports. Source authoring files are requested, not produced by this procedural study. Four named groups: `01_far`, `02_mid`, `03_play`, `04_fore`; keep optional A-loop and B-response frames separate. Never flatten collision cues, gameplay labels, player art or UI into a background.

Work against supplied **1280×720 native 1x captures** and the numerical collision/reservation report, not a resized overview. Native world pixels are the unit: one art pixel equals one gameplay pixel at zoom 1. Preserve the approved Kaze scale from the reference. Room-sized backing plates may use actual pinned bounds (R0/R4/R14: 2600×1440; R16: 2000×1440), but do not treat those bounds as final commissioning sizes until Level accepts geometry. Add overscan only after the camera owner specifies the real travel/framing envelope; no arbitrary connector placement or invented panorama extension.

Export transparent layers with straight alpha, identical canvas/pivot across animation frames, no colored matte fringe and no lossy compression. Provide alpha-union bounds and bottom-center placement pivot for finite props. Use linear filtering for detailed character/background consistency; a nearest-filter comparison may be reviewed, never globally adopted automatically. Motion loops require a documented cadence and loop seam; ART11's 12fps review assumption does not establish the original source frame rate.

## Readability masks and handoff format

Supply one placement record per candidate: room ID; pinned source SHA; layer; actual world rectangle/pivot; atlas/frame path; alpha-union bounds including maximum sway; blend/filter setting; material ID; A/B/C category; narrative certainty; author/license evidence; creator approval ID (initially null). Attach reference screenshots privately. Never upload raw packs or derivative pixels to Git.

Keep original top rims, player feet, landing silhouette, anchors, pickups, hazard warning/active limits, enemy attack arcs, exit plaques, checkpoint approach, lore read and transition framing out of opaque foreground. The current native mask margins are conservative review bounds, not approved gameplay hitboxes. Any need to shrink them returns to the proper owner; art never silently changes collision or hides the conflict.

## Approval sequence

1. Creator chooses room mood/material density and approves or rejects each illustration brief.
2. Level locks the four affected geometry/camera masks, then separately addresses return/fire/connector proposals.
3. Story accepts any optional prop or chronology implication individually; D1–D14 are not blanket-approved.
4. Art Mane paints the selected layers and supplies provenance/alpha/frame metadata privately.
5. Environmental/Gameplay owners review 1x art-on/off, native movement, hazard/enemy visibility and performance; rights owner resolves DarkCave and packaging.
6. PM/creator explicitly authorize any later production integration. This draft stops before that step.
