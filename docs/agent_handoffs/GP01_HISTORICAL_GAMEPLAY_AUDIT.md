## Swimming restoration proposal — not implemented

Use a new opt-in `scripts/water_zone.gd` Area2D with enter/exit/surface interface, and a separate water-motion module called only when submerged. Affected current files would be player.gd (water-state dispatch/reset/allowed actions), world.gd (respawn/recovery/room-boundary resets and water placement), world_expansion.gd or a separately approved aquatic room scene (volumes and dry recovery), hud.gd (swim control hints), and new swimming/transition tests. Input bindings would add explicit up/down swim without replacing dry jump/ground controls.

Keep dry `_physics_process`, dash, wall jump, current swing integrator and sprite scales unchanged outside water. Historical230/180/1000/600 values are proposals, not approved current balance. Decide swim jump stroke/surface exit, dash behavior, submerged tether restriction, elemental attacks, progression unlock, save persistence and aquatic route before implementation. Prefer transient water-state detection; do not persist physical volume references in saves.

Risks: current water occupies drawn hazard bands without Areas; existing fall/recovery may fight buoyancy; hand offset and capsule differ from historical scene; overlap leave events/room teardown can strand state; new input aliases can trigger jumps at surfaces; changing animation mapping would violate current preservation checks. New water geometry/route belongs to Level Design, presentation to Art; PM approval is required for this substantial addition.

Required tests: overlapping volumes; neutral hover;30/60/120Hz ascent/descent; surface jump/exit; dash/tether/kunai/combat underwater according to approved rules; enter while falling or dashing; leave/respawn/load/portal reset; safe dry ledges and reciprocal exits; dry differential controller suite unchanged; manual swimming feel and60FPS GPU rendering measurement.

## Remaining capability gaps

Crouch and slide are absent in both inspected controllers. Traversal artwork contains poses but no implemented crouch collision resize or slide motion state. Mark these FAIL/absent against the assigned capability check; proposing a verified plan is not implementation. Add them only with explicit input, capsule clearance, timing and progression decisions. Do not claim animation frames establish working mechanics.

Current rooms have recovery floors and automated arrival/landing probes; falls still recover within the room rather than transitioning to a lower screen. No global softlock-free campaign or fully continuous subterranean descent is claimed. Subjective web parity, character proportions beyond locked sprites, encounter pacing and60FPS rendering remain manual review requirements.
